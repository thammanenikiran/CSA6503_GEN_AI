HOSTEL AND CAMPUS LIFE - GREENFIELD COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Hostel Facilities
The college operates separate hostels for men and women, each with a capacity of 800 students. Rooms are offered in double and single-sharing configurations, all equipped with Wi-Fi, study tables, and attached bathrooms. Each hostel block has a common recreation room, a gymnasium, and 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance and biometric entry.

Mess and Food
The hostel mess provides breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner on a rotating weekly menu, with separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian counters. Special meals are arranged during festivals. Students with specific dietary requirements can register with the hostel warden for customized meal plans.

Hostel Rules
- In-time for entry into the hostel is 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:30 PM on weekends; late entries require prior written permission from the warden.
- Overnight outings require an approved outpass signed by a parent/guardian and the warden.
- Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable under the UGC anti-ragging regulations, including possible expulsion.
- Visitors are permitted only in the designated visitor's lounge during visiting hours (4:00 PM - 6:00 PM).

Clubs and Extracurricular Activities
The college has over 20 active student clubs, including the Coding Club, Robotics Club, Literary Club, Music and Dance Club, and the Entrepreneurship Cell. The annual technical symposium "TechnoVista" and cultural fest "Zephyr" are organized every year in February and September respectively, attracting participation from over 40 colleges across the state.

Sports Facilities
The campus has a cricket ground, basketball and volleyball courts, an indoor badminton hall, and a gymnasium. The college fields teams in inter-collegiate tournaments and has a dedicated Sports Quota admission scheme for state and national level athletes.
