FEE STRUCTURE AND SCHOLARSHIPS - GREENFIELD COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Tuition Fees (Per Annum, 2026-27)
- B.Tech (all branches): INR 1,25,000
- M.Tech: INR 90,000
- MBA: INR 1,50,000
Hostel fees (including mess charges) are billed separately at INR 85,000 per annum for double-sharing rooms and INR 1,05,000 for single rooms.

Payment Schedule
Tuition fees may be paid in two installments: the first at the time of admission and the second before the start of the even semester. A late payment fine of INR 100 per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of 15 days, beyond which the student's registration may be cancelled.

Scholarships Available
1. Merit Scholarship: Students scoring above 90% in the qualifying examination receive a 25% tuition fee waiver for the first year, renewable each year based on maintaining a CGPA above 8.5.
2. Need-Based Scholarship: Available to students with family income below INR 3,00,000 per annum, covering up to 50% of tuition fees, subject to submission of income certificates.
3. Sports Scholarship: State or national-level sports achievers can receive up to 100% tuition waiver, decided by the Sports Committee.
4. Government Scholarships: The college facilitates applications for state and central government scholarships such as the Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC students.

Refund Policy
If a student withdraws admission before the commencement of classes, 90% of the tuition fee is refunded. Withdrawal within 15 days of course commencement results in a 50% refund. No refund is issued after 15 days from the commencement of classes, in line with UGC/AICTE refund guidelines.
