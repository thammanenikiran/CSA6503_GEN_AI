ADMISSIONS POLICY - GREENFIELD COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Eligibility Criteria
Candidates seeking admission to the B.Tech program must have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, securing a minimum of 60% aggregate marks (55% for reserved categories). Admission to the M.Tech program requires a valid B.Tech/B.E. degree in a relevant discipline with at least 60% marks or a CGPA of 6.5 out of 10.

Entrance Examinations
Undergraduate admissions are based on the candidate's rank in the State Common Entrance Test (CET) or the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main). Postgraduate admissions require a valid GATE score. The college also reserves 15% of seats for management quota candidates, who are admitted based on merit within that category.

Application Process
Applications open in the last week of April and close in the third week of June every academic year. Candidates must apply through the official online admission portal by submitting scanned copies of their mark sheets, transfer certificate, migration certificate, and category certificate (if applicable). The non-refundable application fee is INR 1,000 for general category and INR 500 for SC/ST/PWD candidates.

Counseling and Seat Allotment
Counseling is conducted in three rounds. Candidates must report to the admission cell with original documents within 3 working days of seat allotment to confirm their seat. Failure to report within the stipulated time will result in forfeiture of the allotted seat, which will then be offered to the next candidate in the merit list.
