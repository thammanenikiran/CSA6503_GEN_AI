EXAMINATION RULES AND LIBRARY POLICY - GREENFIELD COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Examination Pattern
Each semester has two Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) tests worth 15 marks each, an assignment/quiz component worth 10 marks, and a Semester End Examination (SEE) worth 60 marks, for a total of 100 marks per subject. A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory to be eligible to sit for the SEE; students with 65-74% attendance may apply for condonation with a valid medical certificate and a fine of INR 500.

Passing Criteria
Students must secure a minimum of 40% in the SEE and 50% in the aggregate (internal + external) to pass a subject. Students who fail a subject can appear for the arrear examination conducted at the end of the following semester.

Revaluation and Retotaling
Students may apply for retotaling within 7 days of result announcement for a fee of INR 300 per subject, or for revaluation for INR 500 per subject. Revaluation results are typically declared within 21 days.

Examination Malpractice
Any student found using unfair means during an examination will have that semester's result withheld pending inquiry by the Discipline Committee. Confirmed cases of malpractice can result in penalties ranging from a zero in that subject to debarment for up to two semesters.

Library Policy
The Central Library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days and 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays. Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for a period of 14 days; postgraduate students and faculty may borrow up to 8 books for 30 days. A fine of INR 2 per day per book applies for overdue returns. The library also provides access to IEEE Xplore, Springer, and ScienceDirect digital databases through the campus network.
