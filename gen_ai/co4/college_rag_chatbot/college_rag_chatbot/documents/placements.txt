PLACEMENT CELL - GREENFIELD COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Placement Statistics (2025-26)
Overall placement rate stood at 87% across all branches, with the Computer Science and Engineering department recording 96% placement. The highest package offered was INR 42 lakhs per annum (international offer), while the average package across the college was INR 6.8 lakhs per annum. Over 150 companies visited the campus during the placement season.

Eligibility for Placement Drives
Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 6.0 with no standing backlogs to be eligible to sit for core company placement drives. Some product-based and analytics companies set a higher cutoff of 7.5 CGPA. Students are permitted a maximum of 3 dream company attempts even after accepting an offer from a regular recruiter.

Training and Preparation
The Training and Placement Cell conducts mandatory aptitude training, group discussion practice, and mock interview sessions starting from the 5th semester. Pre-placement talks are scheduled a week before each company's visit. The college has tie-ups with online learning platforms for coding practice and soft-skills certification.

Major Recruiters
Recurring recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Zoho, Accenture, Amazon, and several product startups in the AI/analytics space. Core companies such as L&T, Ashok Leyland, and Caterpillar regularly recruit from the Mechanical and Civil departments.

Internship Support
The Placement Cell also facilitates summer and semester-long internships, many of which convert into pre-placement offers (PPOs). Students are encouraged to complete at least one internship before their final year.
