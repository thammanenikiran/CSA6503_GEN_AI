# Hydraulic Press HP-50 — System Guide

## Overview
The HP-50 is a 50-tonne hydraulic press. A 5.5 kW motor drives a gear pump that
supplies oil to a single-acting cylinder. System relief pressure is set to
180 bar. The recommended hydraulic oil is ISO VG 46.

## Normal Operating Values
- System pressure at full load: 160 - 180 bar
- Oil temperature: 40 - 55 degrees C
- Reservoir level: between the MIN and MAX marks on the sight glass
- Oil cleanliness: ISO 18/16/13 or cleaner

## Common Faults and Causes
- **Press will not build full pressure:** low oil level, a stuck-open relief
  valve, a worn pump, or internal leakage past the cylinder seals.
- **Slow ram movement:** air trapped in the cylinder, low oil level, a partly
  blocked filter, or oil that is too cold and viscous.
- **Oil overheating (above 60 degrees C):** the relief valve continuously
  discharging, a clogged cooler, or low oil level. Overheated oil oxidises and
  varnishes the valves.
- **Ram drifts down when idle:** internal leakage across the directional valve
  or worn cylinder seals.
- **Foaming oil / noisy pump:** air leaking into the suction line or water
  contamination. Check the suction hose clamps and the oil condition.

## Maintenance Schedule
- Daily: check oil level and oil temperature; look for leaks.
- Monthly: inspect and clean the return filter.
- Every 2000 hours: sample the oil for contamination.
- Every 4000 hours: replace the return and suction filters; change the oil if
  the ISO cleanliness target is not met.

## Safety
Lower the ram and release system pressure to zero before any maintenance.
Support the ram mechanically before working underneath it.
