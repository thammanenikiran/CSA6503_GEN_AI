# Centrifugal Pump — Operation & Maintenance Guide

## Overview
Model CP-200 is a single-stage centrifugal pump used to move cooling water in
the plant. Rated flow is 200 litres per minute at a discharge pressure of
4.5 bar. The pump is driven by a 7.5 kW three-phase induction motor at
2900 RPM.

## Normal Operating Values
- Discharge pressure: 4.0 - 4.8 bar
- Suction pressure: -0.2 to 0.3 bar
- Bearing temperature: below 80 degrees C
- Maximum vibration: 4.5 mm/s RMS

## Common Faults and Causes
- **Low discharge pressure:** usually caused by a clogged suction strainer,
  a closed or partly open suction valve, worn impeller, or air leaking into
  the suction line (loss of prime).
- **Cavitation (rattling / gravel noise):** low suction pressure or high fluid
  temperature reduces Net Positive Suction Head. Reduce suction lift, clean the
  strainer, or lower the fluid temperature.
- **Mechanical seal leakage:** worn seal faces or a dry-run event. The
  mechanical seal must be replaced every 8000 operating hours.
- **Excess vibration:** misalignment between pump and motor shafts, a bent
  shaft, or worn bearings.

## Maintenance Schedule
- Weekly: check discharge pressure and record bearing temperature.
- Monthly: inspect the mechanical seal for drips and clean the suction strainer.
- Every 4000 hours: re-grease bearings with lithium-based grease.
- Every 8000 hours: replace the mechanical seal and check impeller clearance.

## Safety
Always isolate and lock out the motor before opening the pump casing. Never run
the pump dry, as this destroys the mechanical seal within seconds.
