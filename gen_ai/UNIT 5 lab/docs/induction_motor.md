# Three-Phase Induction Motor — Troubleshooting Guide

## Overview
Motor IM-75 is a 7.5 kW, 415 V, three-phase squirrel-cage induction motor
running at 2900 RPM. Full-load current is 14.5 A. Insulation class F.

## Normal Operating Values
- Full-load current: 14 - 15 A per phase, balanced within 5 percent
- Winding temperature: below 120 degrees C (class F limit is 155 C)
- Bearing temperature: below 90 degrees C
- Insulation resistance: above 1 megaohm (measured with a 500 V megger)

## Common Faults and Causes
- **Motor overheating:** overload, phase imbalance, a blocked cooling fan,
  high ambient temperature, or too many starts per hour. Check the load
  current on all three phases first.
- **Motor will not start / hums:** single phasing (one phase lost), a tripped
  overload relay, or a jammed driven load. Never leave a humming motor
  energised; it draws locked-rotor current and overheats quickly.
- **Tripping the overload relay:** sustained overload, an incorrect relay
  setting, or a bearing beginning to seize.
- **Bearing noise or vibration:** worn or dry bearings. Re-grease every 4000
  hours; replace bearings that run above 90 degrees C.
- **Low insulation resistance:** moisture ingress or aged insulation. Dry the
  windings and re-test before energising.

## Maintenance Schedule
- Monthly: measure and log the current on all three phases.
- Every 4000 hours: re-grease the bearings.
- Every 8000 hours: megger-test the insulation and clean the cooling fan.

## Safety
Lock out and tag out the supply before any work. Confirm zero voltage with a
tester before touching the terminals.
